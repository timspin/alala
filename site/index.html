About

Hello World! We are ALALA!

ALALA is an independent multi-genre music platform that was born in Almaty, Kazakhstan.

ALALA is a small group of enthusiasts that strive to share and collect some good music, appreciate wild dances, and believe in the unity of like-minded people.

We are hoping to spread joyful vibes for every listener that is tuning to our platform.

Tune in and Enjoy! Best, ALALA
